Privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026. This covers the Open edX Multi-tenant demo apps for iOS and Android and this website. Raccoon Gang runs both.

The demo apps

  • When the app opens, it downloads the public list of platforms from https://multi-tenant.stepanok.com/instances.json. That request carries nothing about you.
  • When you pick a platform and sign in, your username and password go straight to that platform, the Open edX site you chose. They never reach us.
  • Your courses, progress, messages and downloaded videos come from that platform and are kept on your device. That platform's own privacy policy covers them.
  • The demo apps send us nothing else: no analytics, no crash reports, no push notifications, no advertising identifiers.
  • Removing the app removes what it stored on your device. On iPhone, sign out first: iOS keeps saved sign-in tokens in the Keychain after an app is removed.

This website and the platform registry

  • If you create an account to add a platform, we store your name, your email address, a hash of your password, and the platform details you enter: its name, address, OAuth client ID, colors and logo.
  • Platform details are public: they appear in the catalog and in both demo apps. Your name and email are not published.
  • We use your email only to let you sign in and, if your listing needs attention, to contact you about it. We don't sell it or share it.
  • The server keeps short-term access logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to keep the service secure.

Removing your data

You can remove a platform at any time from your workspace; it leaves the catalog straight away, and the apps stop listing it the next time they open. To have your account deleted, contact Raccoon Gang through raccoongang.com/contact-us and mention the Open edX Multi-tenant demo.
